# Banking Structure, Payments and Institutions

## Types of banks

| Bank | Key rules |
|---|---|
| **Public sector banks** | **12** after the mergers of 1 April 2020 (10 PSBs merged into 4); earlier, Dena and Vijaya Bank merged into Bank of Baroda (1 April 2019) and SBI's associates into SBI (1 April 2017). Heads are selected by the **FSIB** (Financial Services Institutions Bureau, 2022, which replaced the Banks Board Bureau) |
| **Private sector banks** | Licensed under Sec 22 of the BR Act; promoter holding capped at 26% in the long run |
| **Foreign banks** | Operate as branches or as wholly owned subsidiaries |
| **Regional Rural Banks** | RRB Act, 1976 (first RRBs on 2 October 1975, e.g., Prathama Bank, Moradabad). Shareholding: Centre 50%, sponsor bank 35%, State 15%. Under "One State, One RRB", their number fell to **28** from 1 May 2025 |
| **Small finance banks** | Guidelines 2014; on-tap licensing 2019; minimum paid-up capital **₹200 crore**; **60%** priority sector target; at least half the loan book in loans up to **₹25 lakh**; CRAR 15% |
| **Payments banks** | Minimum capital **₹100 crore**; deposits up to **₹2 lakh** per customer; **no loans or credit cards**; at least 75% of demand deposits in SLR securities of up to 1 year and up to 25% with other banks; can apply to become an SFB after 5 years |
| **Local Area Banks** | Small private banks (1996) serving up to three contiguous districts |
| **Urban co-operative banks** | Four-tier regulation by deposit size (Tier 1 up to ₹100 crore … Tier 4 above ₹10,000 crore); an umbrella organisation (NUCFDC) supports them |

- **SFB to universal bank** (April 2024 guidelines): the SFB must be listed, have net worth of at least **₹1,000 crore**, meet capital norms, have made net profit in the last two years, keep **gross NPA ≤ 3% and net NPA ≤ 1%** for two years, and have a satisfactory track record of at least 5 years.
- **NBFCs** follow **scale-based regulation** (Base, Middle, Upper and Top layers). From 24 June 2026 an NBFC with assets of **₹1 lakh crore or more** is in the Upper Layer.
- **All-India Financial Institutions** regulated by RBI: EXIM Bank, NABARD, NHB, SIDBI and **NaBFID** (National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development, 2021).

## Payment and settlement systems

- Governed by the **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**. **NPCI** (2008, a not-for-profit company promoted by banks) runs most retail payment systems.

| System | Operator | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| **RTGS** | RBI | Real-time gross settlement; minimum **₹2 lakh**, no maximum; **24×7×365 since 14 December 2020** |
| **NEFT** | RBI | Deferred net settlement in **48 half-hourly batches**; no minimum or maximum; **24×7 since 16 December 2019** |
| **IMPS** | NPCI | Instant 24×7 transfers, up to **₹5 lakh** |
| **UPI** | NPCI | Launched 2016; generally ₹1 lakh per transaction; **₹5 lakh** for tax payments, hospitals and education, IPOs and RBI Retail Direct, and some other notified categories |
| **UPI Lite** | NPCI | Small-value on-device wallet – up to ₹1,000 per payment, balance up to ₹5,000 |
| **UPI 123PAY** | NPCI | UPI for feature phones – up to ₹10,000 per transaction |
| CTS | NPCI | Cheque clearing by image; continuous clearing with on-realisation settlement since 4 October 2025 |
| NACH | NPCI | Bulk and repetitive payments – salaries, dividends, EMIs, subsidies |
| AePS | NPCI | Aadhaar-enabled payments at micro-ATMs and BCs |
| Bharat Connect (BBPS) | NPCI (NBBL) | Bill payments |
| NETC FASTag | NPCI | Electronic toll collection |
| RuPay | NPCI | Domestic card scheme; RuPay credit cards can be linked to UPI |

- **Charges:** online NEFT and RTGS are **free** for savings account customers. At branches, banks may charge at most ₹2.50 (NEFT up to ₹10,000), ₹5 (above ₹10,000 to ₹1 lakh), ₹15 (above ₹1 lakh to ₹2 lakh) and ₹25 (above ₹2 lakh); RTGS ₹24.50 (₹2–5 lakh) and ₹49.50 (above ₹5 lakh), plus GST.
- **LEI** is needed for RTGS and NEFT payments of **₹50 crore and above** by entities.
- **Prepaid payment instruments (PPIs):** small PPIs with minimum KYC; full-KYC PPIs up to **₹2 lakh** outstanding.
- **e-RUPI** (2021) – a purpose-specific digital voucher. **Digital rupee (e₹)** – RBI's CBDC, wholesale pilot from 1 November 2022 and retail pilot from 1 December 2022.
- **RBI Digital Payments Index** (base March 2018 = 100) uses five weighted parameters: payment enablers (25%), payment infrastructure – demand side (10%) and supply side (15%), payment performance (45%) and consumer centricity (5%).

## ATMs and cash

- **White Label ATMs** (run by non-banks) since 2012.
- **Free transactions:** 5 a month at the customer's own bank's ATMs; at other banks' ATMs 3 in the six metro centres and 5 elsewhere. Beyond these, the customer can be charged up to **₹23** per transaction (from 1 May 2025).
- **Failed ATM withdrawal:** the money must be auto-reversed within **T + 5 days**; for delay, the bank pays **₹100 a day** to the customer.
- **₹100 / ₹200 notes:** 90% of ATMs must dispense them from at least one cassette (by 31 March 2026).
- **Currency chests** are run by banks as RBI's agents. The ₹2,000 note was withdrawn from circulation in May 2023 but remains legal tender.

## Financial inclusion

- **Business Correspondent (BC) model** (2006) – agents provide banking services in villages; every village is to have a banking touch point within 5 km.
- **RBI Financial Inclusion Index:** 53.9 (March 2021), 56.4 (2022), 60.1 (2023), 64.2 (2024), 67.0 (2025) and **70.0 (March 2026)**. It has three sub-indices – **Access (35%)**, **Usage (45%)** and **Quality (20%)** – and ranges from 0 (complete exclusion) to 100 (full inclusion).

## Official language policy (for public sector banks)

- Governed by the **Official Languages Act, 1963** and the **Official Language Rules, 1976**. States and UTs are grouped into regions **A, B and C**.
- Letters received in Hindi must be answered in Hindi (Rule 5). **Hindi Divas** is observed on **14 September**.
- Annual targets for correspondence in Hindi: A to A and A to B – 100%; B to A and B to B – 90%; A to C – 65%; C to A, B or C and B to C – 55%.

## Quick recap

- 12 PSBs; 28 RRBs from 1 May 2025; RRB shares 50:35:15.
- SFB: ₹200 crore, 60% PSL, half the loans ≤ ₹25 lakh; payments bank: ₹100 crore, ₹2 lakh deposits, no loans.
- RTGS min ₹2 lakh (24×7 since December 2020); NEFT 48 batches (24×7 since December 2019); IMPS ₹5 lakh; UPI ₹1 lakh (₹5 lakh notified uses).
- ATM: ₹23 per extra transaction; failed withdrawal T+5, then ₹100 a day.
- FI-Index 70.0 (March 2026); weights 35/45/20.

## One-liners for the exam hall

- RBI started 1 April 1935; nationalised 1 January 1949; Sec 24 – notes up to ₹10,000.
- Inflation target 4% (2–6%) for April 2026 – March 2031; MPC minutes on day 14.
- Repo 5.50%, SDF 5.25%, MSF 5.75%, CRR 3%, SLR 18%.
- Substantial interest – over ₹2 crore or 10% of capital; RBI approval for 5% acquisition (Sec 12B).
- Unclaimed deposits – DEA Fund after 10 years; interest 3% simple.
- Cheque validity – 3 months; Sec 138: 30 days notice, 15 days to pay, 1 month to complain.
- Interim compensation (143A) and appeal deposit (148) – 20% each.
- Up to 4 nominees; lockers and safe custody – successive nomination only.
- KYC updation – 2/8/10 years; CTR above ₹10 lakh by the 15th; STR within 7 working days.
- Small account – ₹50,000 balance; ₹1 lakh credits a year; ₹10,000 withdrawals a month.
- RB-IOS 2026 – ₹30 lakh + ₹3 lakh; complain to the bank first and wait 30 days.
- Electronic fraud – report within 3 working days for zero liability; shadow credit in 10 working days.
- Document return after loan closure – 30 days, else ₹5,000 a day.
- Locker – bank liable up to 100 times the annual rent.
- PSL – 40/18/10/7.5/12; RRB 75%; SFB and UCB 60%.
- Education loan PSL ₹25 lakh; housing ₹50/45/35 lakh; social infrastructure ₹8 crore; renewable energy ₹35 crore.
- MSME – micro ₹2.5 crore / ₹10 crore; small ₹25 crore / ₹100 crore; medium ₹125 crore / ₹500 crore.

- CGTMSE – up to ₹10 crore; 85% / 75% cover; lock-in 18 months.
